# ML Algorithm Audit — Errors Found and Fixed (2026-11-11)

Pre-beta audit of every ML/statistical algorithm: all 22 modules in
`src/analysis/`, the four scorers in `src/scoring/`, `src/backtesting/`,
`src/tracking/performance.py`, and the model wiring in
`src/pipeline/orchestrator.py` and `src/api/api.py`.

Follows `docs/ML_MODEL_REVIEW.md` (2026-06-10). The fixes from that review
were re-checked and hold (section C).

**How findings were established.** Three kinds of evidence, marked in the tables:

- **DB** — seen in live data (`smart_money.db`: 4,652 events, read-only queries)
- **SYN** — reproduced by running the project's own function on synthetic input
- **READ** — by code reading only

**Cut-over.** Rows written before this change keep the old logic. Stored
ensemble scores, options rows and event studies from before 2026-11-11 are
not rewritten; where the definition changed, new rows are marked
(`options_flow.method_version = 2`, `event_study_results.anchor =
'disclosure'`) and old rows are ignored by the new code.

**Who sees model output.** The daily digest and beta email are rule-based and
use no model scores (`src/alerts/digest.py`). Model output reaches beta
testers through the dashboard, the API and the Slack/WhatsApp analysis
verdicts.

## A. Errors in the live path — all fixed

### XGBoost classifier (not yet trained — no model file exists)

Fixed before first training:

- Training features came from each ticker's **latest** model rows, computed after the outcome. Now taken as of the signal (`api._build_xgb_record`).
- Unpriced signals and (by default) 13F rows are excluded from training.
- Imputation and scaling moved inside the model pipeline (fit per CV fold). The L1-logistic path previously ran on unscaled features.
- CV folds are purged of training rows whose 20-day label window reaches the test fold.
- The "better than null" gate also requires the CV AUC to clear 0.5 by one standard error.
- One feature builder (`analysis/xgb_features.py`) for training, prediction and ticker analysis; unknowns stay missing instead of 0.

## B. Unwired modules — not fixed

Nothing imports `structural_breaks`, `granger_causality`,
`conformal_prediction`, `adversarial_validation`, `ic_monitor`,
`correlation_filter`, `WalkForwardCalibrator`, `RegimeConditionalCalibrator`
or `apply_fdr_filter`, so they do not affect output. Treat them as
experimental. Known problems to fix before wiring any of them in:

- `structural_breaks`: the "recent break" flag can never fire (**SYN** 0 of 50 injected breaks) while pure noise yields ~5 "breaks" per series; the CUSUM threshold is far beyond the 5% critical value; Bai–Perron is named but not implemented.
- `granger_causality`: the significant-fraction denominator includes tickers that were never tested; buys and sells share one unsigned indicator.
- `apply_fdr_filter`: a p-value of exactly 0.0 is treated as missing; signals without an event study are always downgraded.
- `conformal_prediction`: "empirical coverage" is measured on the calibration set itself.
- `ic_monitor`: overlapping windows overstate ICIR.
- `correlation_filter`: clusters chain-link across months; return histories are tail-aligned.
- `WalkForwardCalibrator`: no embargo between train and test months; `RegimeConditionalCalibrator` fails on a `None` regime.

## C. Checked and correct

Monte Carlo drift and fat-tail scaling; t-copula likelihood and
tail-dependence formula; event-study market model, post-event CAR, t-stat and
bootstrap; Fama–French date join and OLS; macro modifier; HMM BIC parameter
count; conformal quantile index; Ledoit–Wolf use; news look-ahead guard and
timezones; congressional window on publication date; direction-adjusted
return labels.

## D. What the fixes do not establish

- **Predictive value.** The repaired components are correct by construction;
  none has been shown to predict returns. Only ~200 matured insider and
  congressional outcomes exist, spread over 29 distinct entry days.
- **Conviction.** In live data conviction does not rank outcomes (buys:
  Spearman −0.07; passing vs failing the threshold −3.6% vs −2.3% at 20
  days). Dominated by the 13F cluster — re-measure now that 13F is separated.
- **Monte Carlo drift** is a raw two-year sample mean (annual drift p1 −257%,
  p99 +125%), i.e. trailing momentum. Left as is; a strategy decision.
- **Pooled event study and decay need history.** They stay absent for a
  source × direction until 20 distinct disclosure dates have matured.
  13F filings cluster on about four dates a year and will not qualify.
- **Unpriced signals are excluded, not counted as losses.** This removes the
  invented ±100% rows but can hide a real bankruptcy. The count is reported
  (`unpriced_signals`) so the exclusion is visible.
- **Fixed-threshold labels in the dashboard options panel** (put/call
  "Bullish / Bearish") are display conventions; the model no longer uses them.

## E. What changes for someone looking at the output

- **Far fewer actionable calls.** With the inflation removed, totals cluster
  near 50. On the 102 most recent signals, 6 are rated buy/sell after the
  fixes versus 39 before (median total 48.6, p95 57.4, max 67.0). The action
  cut-off (55) was left alone; the "strong" cut-off moved from 75 (never
  reached by any of 4,621 stored scores) to 65, on a thin one-day sample —
  re-derive it after a few weeks of new scores.
- **The HMM regime is "sideways" for most tickers** (50 of 59 in the sample)
  and then abstains. Daily-return HMM states differ in volatility far more
  than in drift; the earlier bull/bear labels were mostly noise.
- **Event study and decay are absent today.** No source × direction has 20
  matured disclosure dates yet. Insider buys reach 21 once the backlog of
  past signals has been measured (about three pipeline runs at 500 tickers
  each); on a copy with the backlog drained their pooled 20-day CAR is +3.5%
  (p = 0.17 across dates), decay half-life 19 days (95% interval 5–131).
- **Weather is absent for Financials and Industrials** until 10 days of
  alert counts have been stored.
- **GARCH sits slightly below 50 for the median ticker** (forecast/realized
  ratio median 1.10): forecasts revert toward a long-run level that is above
  the recent 60 days. It moves totals by about half a point and does not vote.
- **Old rows stay as they were.** Signals scored before the cut-over keep
  their old ensemble scores until something re-scores them.

### Environment gap found during verification

The scheduled job runs in `.venv`, which has no `emcee` and cannot load
`xgboost` (no OpenMP runtime). Consequences and what was done:

- **Decay could not be fitted there.** Without `emcee` it returns nothing
  (never a made-up number). `emcee` was in `requirements.txt` but missing
  from `requirements-lock.txt`; it has been added to the lock file and
  installed in `.venv` (3.1.6, 2026-11-11). The 17:30 run on that day
  predates the install, so its 69 signals have no decay component.
- **The classifier** now falls back to the logistic model when `xgboost`
  cannot load, instead of refusing to train.

## G. Where the work departed from the approved plan

- **Security type.** Yahoo's `quoteType` is `EQUITY` for notes and preferred
  shares too, so classification also uses the security name and symbol.
- **Unpriced signals** are excluded rather than booked at a loss; a history
  that ends early settles at its last traded price.
- **Classifier features** are taken up to one day after the signal was
  stored (model rows are written minutes after ingestion), not strictly
  before it.
- **Options percentiles** rank against all tickers scored with the current
  method in the last 30 days (own history only when fewer than 20 exist).
- **HMM** gained a change that was not in the plan: significance-based
  labels and 10 starts (finding A3 b, c).
- **Event-study batch** is 500 tickers per run rather than 150.
- **Not done:** the unwired modules in section B. Stored conviction scores
  were not recomputed (only the title-matching fix affects them).
